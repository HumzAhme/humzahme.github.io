- Developed daily catchment-average precipitation and temperature inputs from DWD HYRAS data and calculated evapotranspiration using the Hargreaves–Samani method.
- Calibrated and compared GR4J, HBV, and GR6J using SCE-UA and a swapped split-sample design, then simulated daily streamflow for the ungauged 2011–2020 period.
- Quantified uncertainty from parameter sets, model structure, calibration period, and input datasets; GR6J performed best by NSE (0.70–0.73), while rankings varied by performance metric.
- Conducted a counterfactual climate-change analysis, estimating ~15% lower summer discharge due to observed warming and drying trends.
