- 📡 Analyzed UAV and terrestrial LiDAR data to measure vegetation structure in forest and plantation research, and supported field experiments on tree stress responses.

📊 𝐋𝐢𝐃𝐀𝐑 & 𝐑𝐞𝐦𝐨𝐭𝐞 𝐒𝐞𝐧𝐬𝐢𝐧𝐠:-
- Built an end-to-end Python pipeline (𝒑𝒐𝒊𝒏𝒕 𝒄𝒍𝒐𝒖𝒅 𝒑𝒓𝒐𝒄𝒆𝒔𝒔𝒊𝒏𝒈 → 𝒕𝒓𝒆𝒆 𝒍𝒐𝒄𝒂𝒍𝒊𝒛𝒂𝒕𝒊𝒐𝒏 → 𝒉𝒆𝒊𝒈𝒉𝒕 𝒆𝒙𝒕𝒓𝒂𝒄𝒕𝒊𝒐𝒏 → 𝒄𝒓𝒐𝒘𝒏 𝒔𝒆𝒈𝒎𝒆𝒏𝒕𝒂𝒕𝒊𝒐𝒏) to detect and measure 444 individually surveyed young conifer seedlings from drone LiDAR
- Compared height estimates across 7 flight-altitude combinations (30 m / 60 m, single and merged flights) using statistical testing (Wilcoxon), showing that 60 m data alone underestimates seedling height
- Processed 𝒕𝒆𝒓𝒓𝒆𝒔𝒕𝒓𝒊𝒂𝒍 𝒍𝒂𝒔𝒆𝒓 𝒔𝒄𝒂𝒏𝒏𝒊𝒏𝒈 (𝑻𝑳𝑺) 𝒂𝒏𝒅 𝑼𝑨𝑽-𝑳𝒊𝑫𝑨𝑹 𝒅𝒂𝒕𝒂 for tree-diversity plots: ground classification, canopy height models, foliage height diversity, and comparison with LAI field measurements 📐
- Applied 𝒘𝒂𝒕𝒆𝒓𝒔𝒉𝒆𝒅 𝒂𝒏𝒅 𝒅𝒆𝒆𝒑-𝒍𝒆𝒂𝒓𝒏𝒊𝒏𝒈 (DeepForest) tree detection and cross-validated the results 
- Ran 𝒑𝒐𝒊𝒏𝒕-𝒅𝒆𝒏𝒔𝒊𝒕𝒚 𝒂𝒏𝒂𝒍𝒚𝒔𝒆𝒔 to choose processing resolution from the data rather than assumptions
- Wrote 𝒗𝒆𝒓𝒊𝒇𝒊𝒄𝒂𝒕𝒊𝒐𝒏 𝒕𝒐𝒐𝒍𝒔 𝒂𝒏𝒅 𝒅𝒐𝒄𝒖𝒎𝒆𝒏𝒕𝒂𝒕𝒊𝒐𝒏  so results can be reproduced and checked

📍𝐅𝐢𝐞𝐥𝐝 𝐰𝐨𝐫𝐤:-
- Flew UAV missions collecting LiDAR and RGB imagery
- Manually verified and corrected detected tree positions in QGIS
- Supported a VOC sampling experiment on Norway spruce under artificial drought (closed-loop bark systems, Gerstel Twisters, 20 trees, Friedrichshöhe)
- Contributed to an experiment on Diplodia fungus effects in pine saplings under normal vs. water-stress conditions, a relevant forest-health issue in Saxony-Anhalt
